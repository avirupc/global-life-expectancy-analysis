# Statistical Analysis

The following tasks describe the statistical analyses to be completed for this project.

- For which income group has the difference in average life expectancy of men and women changed the most between 1960 and 2023?
- For which income group has variability in life expectancy at birth changed the most between 1960 and 2023?
- Which countries have the highest correlation between fertility rate and life expectancy at birth over the years (either positive or negative)? Which countries have the lowest correlation?

I have created the Jupyter Notebook `statistical-analysis.ipynb` for performing these tasks. It contains:

- Code for data loading and preprocessing.
- Implementation of statistical computations. 
- Markdown cells with explanatory notes, assumptions, and rationale for methodological choices.  
- Output cells that display the resulting tables and summaries.

Open `./statistical-analysis.ipynb` to view the code and the results.

Based on the analyses conducted, the following are the answers to the key questions:

<br>

1. **How has the female–male life expectancy gap changed across income groups?**  
   - The analysis shows that the **Upper middle income** group experienced the largest average change in the female–male life expectancy gap between 1960 and 2023.

2. **For which income group has variability in life expectancy at birth changed the most between 1960 and 2023?**  
   - Using standard deviation as a measure of variability, the **Upper middle income** group again shows the greatest change.

3. **Which countries have the highest and lowest correlation between fertility rate and life expectancy at birth over the years?**  
   - Countries with the **highest correlations** (descending order): **India**, Brunei Darussalam, Ghana, Turkiye, Turks and Caicos Islands  

   - Countries with the **lowest correlations** (ascending order): **Belarus**, Eswatini, Central African Republic, Congo, Dem. Rep., Ukraine
